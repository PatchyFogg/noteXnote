noteXnote

Slow it down. Loop it. Learn it.

Quick start: open a song from the Source panel, hit Space to play, and drag the Speed slider down to slow it to whatever tempo you want to play along with, keeping the original pitch if Pitch Lock is on.

Loading a song

The Source panel supports a local audio file, a pasted URL (YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, and 1000+ sites via yt-dlp), or a Spotify link (via spotdl). The folder-shortcut button (📁) next to Open jumps straight to any music folders noteXnote finds on your Mac.

Speed & Pitch Lock

Drag the Speed slider (or type an exact value into its number field) to slow a song down to anywhere from 25% to 200% of its original tempo. With Pitch Lock on, the pitch stays put — a slowed-down riff still sounds like the same riff, not a chipmunk. Under the hood this is WSOLA time-stretching with normalized-correlation splice matching, tuned to avoid the phasey "echo" artifact that cheaper time-stretchers produce on reverb-heavy recordings.

D/A Reconstruction Filter

Every song, automatically: noteXnote runs a simulated analog reconstruction filter, the same kind of stage a real DAC uses to turn digital samples back into a smooth waveform. It's a gentle rolloff above 15kHz that softens the harsh digital edge some recordings carry — no button, no setting, it's just how noteXnote sounds.

Reference Mode

One click on 🎯 Play at 1× (Reference) jumps back to the original, unprocessed recording at full speed — useful for re-anchoring your ear to how the part is actually supposed to sound — then jump right back into practice speed.

A-B Loop & Loop Library

Set A and B points anywhere in the song to loop just that section on repeat. The Loop Library holds up to 5 named loops per song — save the intro, the tricky bridge run, the solo — and jump between them with one click. Everything is remembered per-song, so it's all still there next time you open the same file.

Zoom view

Below the main waveform, the Zoom panel is a DAW-style detail view that scrolls under a fixed center playhead as you play, with a millisecond-precision time ruler. Click or drag inside it to seek with much finer granularity than the overview waveform allows.

EQ — Boost

Four one-click presets — Flat, Bass, Mid, Treble — give a gentle, headroom-safe tonal boost tuned so you'll never hit clipping, no matter how hot the source mix is.

The transport row's ⏮ / ⏭ buttons jump to the start/end of the current A-B loop when one is set, otherwise to the start/end of the song.

Plugins

Plugins are how noteXnote grows without the core app ever changing shape. A plugin becomes “official” via GitHub: a pull request to the noteXnote repo, a real review, and a proper release. This helps to maintain application integrity and choice for users.

Got a released plugin you want to try? Drop its .py file into your plugins folder (Plugins → Open Plugins Folder…) and noteXnote picks it up on its own — no setup, no fuss. Plugins → Manage Plugins… lists everything installed with a quick description and a one-click enable/disable toggle, so you always know exactly what's running and can flip it off just as easily.

Uninstalling

Use Help → Uninstall noteXnote… to remove the app and all of its saved data (preferences, loops, EQ choices, audio cache) in one step.
